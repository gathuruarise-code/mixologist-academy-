Glassware

The right glass supports aroma, temperature, carbonation, presentation and the intended serving size.

Glass capacity varies by manufacturer and venue. Always follow the house standard and leave appropriate room for ice and garnish.

Beer

Beer is brewed from malted grain, water, hops and yeast. Different methods create distinct families and flavours.

06 / SWEETNESS & BALANCE

Syrups

Syrups add sweetness, flavour, texture and consistency. Measure carefully so a drink stays balanced.

PROFESSIONAL STANDARD

Responsible service is part of the craft.

Know and follow local licensing laws, venue policy and legal drinking-age requirements. Never serve an intoxicated guest or encourage excessive consumption. Offer water and non-alcoholic choices, arrange safe transport where needed, and never drink while working.
